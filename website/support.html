Frequently asked questions

Why is the World feed empty?

Only published stories appear in the public feed. Submitted stories stay pending until an editor approves them. The app may show cached or sample stories when offline.

How do I delete my account?

Use the built-in Delete Account feature: open the app → World tab → profile icon → Delete Account. Your data is removed immediately. If you need help, email zub165@yahoo.com. See Delete Account for full details.

How does the Truth Index work?

The AI assigns a score based on story detail, media, and consistency checks. It is a heuristic aid, not a legal verdict. Editors still review all submissions.

How do I sync my to-do checklist?

Sign in with your username and password. Tasks save locally first, then sync to the cloud with a unique client UUID. Tap the cloud icon on the To-Do tab to refresh.

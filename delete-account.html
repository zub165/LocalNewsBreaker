Delete your LocalNewsBreaker account

App: LocalNewsBreaker · Developer: LocalNewsBreaker

LocalNewsBreaker includes a built-in Delete Account feature in the app. You do not need to email us to delete your account — use the steps below.

Delete account in the app (built-in feature)

  1. Open the LocalNewsBreaker app on Android or iPhone.
  2. Go to the World tab.
  3. Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner.
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account (red button).
  5. Your account and profile data are removed from our servers immediately.

This built-in feature calls our API (DELETE /api/v1/users/me/) and clears your local session. No waiting period when you delete from the app.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app, email zub165@yahoo.com from the email address on your account. Include your username. We will delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Username, email, and password (account credentials)
  • Authentication tokens and profile data
  • Synced to-do / release checklist tasks tied to your account
  • Account linkage on your submissions

What may be kept

  • Published news stories may remain in the public feed without personal identifiers.
  • Server backups may be retained up to 90 days, then purged.
  • Local cache on your device is cleared when you uninstall the app or use Delete Account.

Partial data deletion

To remove specific stories without deleting your account, contact zub165@yahoo.com.
